A-03 · Parametric Pavilion

Green Shell

A porous urban pavilion that turns a generative shell into shade, habitat, and public room.

Rhino · Grasshopper

A responsive canopy between city and ecology.

Green Shell brings together organic morphology, digital fabrication logic, and public space. Conceived for Toledo, the pavilion creates an urban oasis where filtered light, shadow, planting, and movement define the experience.

The pavilion is treated as a responsive organism rather than a freestanding object. Its placement and openings establish a localized microclimate while maintaining visual continuity with the surrounding landscape.

A Rhino and Grasshopper workflow transforms a continuous surface into a network of nodes and hollow triangular panels. The porous canopy filters sunlight, frames views, and supports native planting below, making environmental performance legible through form.
