Mobile Bridge

A tiny library that connects river, park, and forest through a productive civic bridge.

A cultural flow belt for a small town.

Hyden, Kentucky faces limited employment, population loss, an aging demographic, and the effects of substance abuse. The proposal responds with a vertically stacked productive space where reading, craft, gardening, health support, and collective activity can coexist.

The concept of a mobile bridge links three natural conditions—the river, park, and forest—into a cultural flow belt. Each end becomes a distinct spatial hub while the center acts as a shared threshold between landscape and town.

Over the river, stepped bookshelves rise toward a viewing level above a glass floor. Toward the forest, reading terraces and a meditation area create quiet visual continuity with the trees. The park-facing zone supports workshops, medical and psychological services, and sustainable agriculture, turning a small library into civic infrastructure.
