AP 3-D Art & Design · 15 works

Atlas of Forced Evolution.

What if animals had to evolve to survive human impact?

This sculptural matrix examines material progress and ecological damage. Each work pairs a vulnerable species with the infrastructure, consumption, entertainment, or waste that pressures its survival.

Return to portfolio

01 · Evolution

8.25 × 8.25 × 4.5 in.
Ceramic, iron chain

A compromised environment forces the gastropod into a grotesque, human-like morphology—a consequence of anthropocentric impact.

02 · Residuum

1.5 × 10 × 10 in.
Ceramic, natural sand, scoop-net, acrylic, found object

A degraded shoreline where an empty oyster shell loses its organic flesh and is left among synthetic maritime refuse.

03 · Decay

2 × 10.5 × 8 in.
Clay, preserved moss, plastic, water bottle, warning sticker

Commercial plastic infiltrates the soil and creates a toxic contrast with living green moss.

04 · Toxification

2.5 × 11.75 × 7 in.
Ceramic, foamboard, collage magazine print

Pustules, scales, and lacerations map fatal aquatic mutations and invasive experimentation.

05 · Delicacy

2.5 × 10 × 10 in.
Ceramic, acrylic paint, porcelain plate, salt, black pepper

Culinary staging masks a mutilated limb, indicting commodification and human desire.

06 · Skin of Envy

5.5 × 8 × 7.25 in.
Ceramic, plastic wrap, barcode

Commercial packaging suffocates the reptile and links snakeskin to luxury consumption.

07 · Echoes of Wrath

8 × 8.5 × 6.75 in.
Ceramic, synthetic plastic rings, acrylic paint

Concentric scars visualize the destructive frequencies of naval sonar imposed on underwater habitats.

08 · Feeding Gluttony

20 × 20 × 14 in.
Cardboard, natural straw, corn, wooden dowels, plastic

A feeding tube turns the animal into a manufacturing asset engineered for accelerated consumption.

09 · Trophy of Greed

23 × 27 × 10 in.
Wooden dowels, acrylic paint, Blender 3D-printed joints

A structural stag head is pierced by arrows, reflecting the pursuit of dominance and trophy status.

10 · Theatre of Pride

8 × 10.25 × 6.5 in.
Ceramic, shaped plastic, acrylic paint

A captive cetacean is turned from an individual life into mass spectacle and entertainment.

11 · Two Seconds of Gravity

6.75 × 9.5 × 6 in.
Ceramic, Blender 3D-printed tire matrix, acrylic paint

Synthetic tire tread conveys urban oppression as the trapped amphibian loses its ability to jump.

12 · Packaged Relic

19.5 × 8.5 × 8.5 in.
Ceramic, foamboard, wooden rod, newspaper, fabric thread

Financial media constructs a poached tusk, showing wildlife converted into commercial capital.

13 · The Rusted Howl

7.25 × 6 × 4.5 in.
Ceramic, industrial steel screws

An apex predator is discarded on urban stone, its evolutionary fangs replaced by machinery.

14 · Infrastructure of Silence

21.25 × 19 × 17.5 in.
Branches, coconut fiber, wire, fractured eggshells

Transmission infrastructure looms over a fragile nest and exposes unseen hazards within local ecosystems.

15 · Imprisoned Freedom

9 × 16 × 23 in.
Wire framework, Blender 3D-printed hand, acrylic

An eagle engineered for open sky is anchored by a human hand—an emblem of forced subjugation.
