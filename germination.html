A-04 · Morphology Study

Germination

A formal system translating the growth logic of white dulcamara into architectural structure.

Biology-to-tectonics translation

Growth made tectonic.

Germination begins with the biological morphology of white dulcamara: an upward trajectory defined by stability, rapid expansion, and unpredictable directional shifts.

Botanical anatomy is mapped to architectural elements. Root becomes pedestal, stem becomes vertical support, branches become cantilevered extensions, and the flower bloom culminates as a spatial volume.

The study does not imitate the plant’s appearance. Instead, it extracts a rule set from growth and tests how organic vitality can meet the discipline of structural geometry.
