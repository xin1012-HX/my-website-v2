Four-cover typology series

Architecture translated into an editorial voice.

This cover series treats editorial design as a form of architectural representation. Each composition combines typographic hierarchy, image cropping, rhythm, and negative space to express a distinct architectural typology.

Urban, residential, interior, and Chinese architecture share a consistent visual system while retaining their own atmosphere. The result is a multidisciplinary exercise in how architecture is framed, sequenced, and communicated to a wider audience.
