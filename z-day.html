Z-Day: Rise of the Horde

A research facility, viral breach, and reactor failure become the spatial climax of a speculative game world.

Narrative built through environment.

Set in 2047 during a high-technology nuclear conflict, Z-Day centers on HelixCore, a research facility developing an anti-radiation vaccine. An explosion breaches containment and releases the volatile Nexis-9 virus.

Lead researcher Barr mutates, damages the reactor, and triggers an escalating fusion failure. Agent 1012 enters the facility in response to a distress signal with two spatial objectives: shut down the reactor and seal the site.

The environment is designed as a sequence of pressure, evidence, and revelation. Lighting, circulation, containment chambers, and industrial scale guide the player toward the Part I climax while establishing the world’s larger themes of nuclear fallout, mutation, and technological arms races.
